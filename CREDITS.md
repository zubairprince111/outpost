# OUTPOST — Asset Credits, Attributions & Open Licenses

This document records all external 3D models, planetary maps, textures, soundscapes, audio tracks, and icons utilized in the **OUTPOST** lunar exploration experience for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

All assets are legal, royalty-free, public domain, or open-licensed (CC0 / CC-BY 4.0 / NASA Open Media).

- **Creator**: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS)
- **Source**: [https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720) (CGI Moon Kit, Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera data)
- **License**: NASA Open Media / Public Domain
- **Included Asset**:
  - `moon_1024.jpg` — Photorealistic lunar regolith albedo map

### NASA Earth Blue Marble & Cloud Textures
- **Creator**: NASA Visible Earth / Goddard Space Flight Center
- **Source**: [https://visibleearth.nasa.gov](https://visibleearth.nasa.gov) / Three.js Planet Examples
- **License**: Public Domain (NASA)
- **Included Assets**:
  - `earth_atmos_2048.jpg` — Photorealistic Blue Marble daytime land and ocean map
  - `earth_clouds_1024.png` — Real satellite-derived atmospheric cloud coverage map
